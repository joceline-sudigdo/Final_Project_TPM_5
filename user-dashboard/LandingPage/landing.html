About

What is Technoscape Hackathon?

"TechnoScape Hackathon 2026 is a prestigious technology competition hosted by BNCC (Bina Nusantara Computer Club), bringing together a golden opportunity for students and young professionals to collaborate, innovate, and compete in creating impactful technology solutions.

With the theme 'Code the Future: AI x Innovation 2026', we invite you to explore the potential of Artificial Intelligence in solving real-world problems. From healthcare, education, sustainability, to smart cities - the challenge awaits you!

For 48 hours non-stop, you'll work in teams, guided by experienced mentors, and judged by professional jury members from leading tech industries. This isn't just a competition - it's a launchpad for your career in the technology world."
